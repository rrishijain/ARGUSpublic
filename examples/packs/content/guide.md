# Content Planning starter pack

All DEMO ideas, owners and January 2026 dates are fictional. Nothing has been scheduled or published to an external account. Use the empty template to supply your own content list.

Confirm audience, business goal, channels, and the difference between planned, scheduled and published. A publishing plan is a draft. Performance analysis needs a separate selected source with defined measurements.

First command: Plan next week's content. Expected demonstration result: 5 ideas, 2 drafts, 1 scheduled item and no inferred performance results. Next builds can include an editorial calendar or content approval tracker.
