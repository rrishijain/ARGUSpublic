# Sales Pipeline starter pack

The DEMO CSV contains fictional January 2026 records. It never represents a connected CRM. `template.csv` contains headers for your own explicitly selected export.

Confirm what `qualified` and `won` mean, the reporting dates, currency for `value`, and whether stage describes current or historical status. Count rows for leads; filter stage explicitly for qualified and won counts. Current-stage counts alone cannot establish a historical conversion funnel. No metrics are activated automatically.

First command: Review my pipeline. Expected demonstration result: 6 leads, 2 currently qualified, 2 won, with the period and limitations stated. Try adding a weekly target tracker after this first review.
